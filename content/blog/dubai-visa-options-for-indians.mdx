Moving to Dubai starts with the right visa. Here are the main routes for Indian professionals.

## Employment visa

The most common route. Your employer sponsors the visa and it covers your Emirates ID, health insurance, and residency. You typically arrive on a visit visa, interview, and convert once you have an offer.

## Freelance permit

Dubai offers a dedicated freelance permit and a digital-nomad visa for remote workers. You can be self-sponsored, useful if you work with clients across borders.

## Investor / business visa

If you're starting a business, free zones like DMCC, IFZA, and Dubai South issue investor visas. Setup costs typically start around AED 10,000.

## Family / dependent visa

If a family member is already a UAE resident, they can sponsor your dependent visa. The process is straightforward once their salary meets the threshold.
