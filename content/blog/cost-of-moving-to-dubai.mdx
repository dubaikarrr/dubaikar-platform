Most people underestimate the cash they need for the first two months. Here's a realistic picture.

## One-time costs

- Flight: AED 600–1,200
- Visit visa: AED 300–600
- Airport taxi, SIM, photocopies: ~AED 200
- Degree attestation: ~AED 500
- Temporary stay while job hunting: AED 80–200/night
- Housing deposit: ~40% of one month's rent

## Monthly costs

- Bed space from AED 725; a 1 BHK can run AED 4,500+
- Food: AED 400 (self-cook) to AED 1,000 (eating out)
- Transport: AED 230 (metro) and up

Add a 10% buffer for surprises. Try the [budget calculator](/tools/budget) to get your own number.
