Once your own residence visa is stamped, you can sponsor your spouse, children and (with conditions) parents. Here's how it works for employees.

## Who can sponsor

- **Minimum salary**: AED 4,000/month (or AED 3,000 + employer accommodation) to sponsor a wife and children. Practically, plan on comfortably more once you cost real housing.
- Both **husbands and wives** can sponsor family (wives sponsoring husbands may need approvals depending on profession/salary).
- **Parents**: possible but stricter, typically AED 20,000/month salary (or lower with two-bedroom housing proof), refundable deposit per parent, and mandatory health insurance.

## Documents you'll need

- Attested **marriage certificate** (for spouse) and **birth certificates** (for children), attested in India through the same MEA → UAE-embassy chain as your degree, then MOFA in the UAE. **Start this early; it's the usual bottleneck.**
- Your tenancy contract (**Ejari**), bed spaces/shared rooms don't qualify; you need a proper tenancy in your name.
- Salary certificate or attested labour contract, passport copies and photos.

## Cost per person (typical)

- Entry permit + status change + residence stamping + Emirates ID: roughly **AED 1,800–2,500** per dependent through standard channels.
- **Medical test** (spouse and 18+): ~AED 320.
- **Health insurance**: mandatory in Dubai, basic plans from ~AED 700–1,500/yr per person; family plans vary widely.
- School-age kids: factor **CBSE/ICSE school fees** (AED 12k–35k/yr at Indian-curriculum schools, see the schools layer on our Dubai map).

## Timeline

With attested certificates in hand, a dependent visa typically completes in **2–4 weeks**: entry permit → (family enters or status-changes in-country) → medical → Emirates ID biometrics → residence stamping.

## Common sequence for movers

1. You land, complete your own visa + Emirates ID, and take a proper tenancy.
2. Certificates get attested in India in parallel.
3. Family follows 1–3 months later, entering on the entry permit.

> Renewals follow the sponsor: dependents' visas are tied to yours, so calendar both expiry dates together.
