Land with a plan and your first week in Dubai is smooth. Here's the sequence that works.

## Day 0, at the airport

- **SIM card**: grab a tourist/new-arrival SIM at the du or e& (Etisalat) counter in arrivals (~AED 100 with data). You need a local number for *everything*, banks, Careem, delivery.
- **Cash**: withdraw AED 500–1,000 from an airport ATM. Many small shops and bed-space landlords prefer cash.
- **Taxi vs Metro**: airport taxis are metered and safe (~AED 50–70 to Bur Dubai). The Red Line metro also runs from Terminals 1 and 3, cheapest option if you land before midnight with light luggage.

## Day 1, transport + bearings

- **NOL card** (metro/bus/tram): buy a **Silver NOL** at any metro station (AED 25, includes AED 19 credit). It also pays for buses and public ferries.
- Walk your neighbourhood: find the nearest metro station, supermarket, and pharmacy.
- Activate **WhatsApp on your UAE number**, landlords and HR coordinate on WhatsApp here.

## Day 2, visa medicals (if employment visa is in process)

Your employer/PRO will book the **medical fitness test** (blood test + chest X-ray, ~AED 320 standard). Carry your passport and visa application copy. Results in 1–3 days; "VIP" same-day options exist.

## Day 3, Emirates ID biometrics

After medicals, you'll do **Emirates ID biometrics** (fingerprints + photo) at an ICP/AMER centre. The physical card arrives in roughly 5–10 days, your employer's PRO usually receives it.

## While you wait

- **Bank account**: shortlist banks (see our bank-account guide); most need your Emirates ID, so prep documents now, passport, visa page, salary certificate from HR.
- **Attestation/MOFA**: if your degree needs the final MOFA stamp, do it this week.
- **Budget reality-check**: track your first-week spend against your saved Dubaikar budget, the first month is always the most expensive.

> Rule of thumb: nothing official moves without your **Emirates ID**. Everything in your first two weeks is about getting it issued fast.
