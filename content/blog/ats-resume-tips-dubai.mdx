Dubai recruiters lean heavily on applicant tracking systems. A few specifics matter more here than back home.

## Always include your visa status

It's the single biggest Dubai-specific filter. State clearly whether you're on a visit visa, employment visa, or need sponsorship.

## Add a job title under your name

A clear target title makes you far more likely to be shortlisted. Match it to the role you're applying for.

## Quantify everything

"Reduced monthly close from 10 to 4 days" beats "responsible for accounts." Numbers survive the ATS and catch the recruiter's eye.

## List 8+ relevant skills

Recruiters filter by skills. Mirror the language in the job description.
